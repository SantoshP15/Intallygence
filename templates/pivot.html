Report Generator
!
To generate your custom report, select respective elements from each column, then press '+' to add your selection. You can select multiple choices in each column. Once you are confirmed of your selection then press 'go'. This will generate a report in user friendly tabular (rows & columns like in excel) format. You can also save the report for future use so you need not repeat the selection steps. Once saved, you can visit 'My Saved Reports' to access your previous reports and delete redundant reports.
